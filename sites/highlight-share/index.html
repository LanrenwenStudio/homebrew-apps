Highlight Share

Mark the passages that matter, keep them organized, and copy links that point people back to the exact context.

Install from Chrome Web Store Read privacy policy

Highlight Share is for readers, researchers, writers, and students who need more than a yellow mark. It keeps selected text tied to the page where it came from, then turns useful passages into clean copy or share links.

What it does

Built around the moment you find something worth keeping.

Select

Highlight without leaving the page.

Select text and save it in place with a compact toolbar. Change colors, copy the selected text, or create a link while the passage is still in view.

Organize

Review every highlight from the side panel.

The side panel groups highlights for the current page, so you can scan, copy, delete, or change the display without digging through the page again.

Share

Send the exact passage, not just the page.

Copy a link that carries the selected passage with it. The person opening it lands with the relevant text highlighted and the context still intact.

Privacy posture

Local-first by default.

Highlights and settings are stored locally in your browser.

Clipboard access is used only when you click a copy or share button.

No personalized ads, no sale of data, and no third-party transfer.
